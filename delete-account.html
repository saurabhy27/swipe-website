Delete your Swipe account

For the Swipe app (expense tracker & SMS money management) · Last updated: 18 August 2026

This page explains how to request that your Swipe account and associated data are permanently deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@saurabh.build from the same email address you use to sign in to Swipe.
  2. Use the subject line: Swipe account deletion request.
  3. Include your Swipe login email (and, if you know it, the name shown on your profile).
  4. We will verify ownership of the account and process the deletion.

Contact: support@saurabh.build

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Signing out of the app only clears local data on that device and does not delete your server account.

What we delete

When your deletion request is processed, we remove from our primary systems:

  • Your account / auth profile (email login)
  • Profile details (display name, avatar if any)
  • Parsed transactions and related ledger data
  • SMS sync records associated with your account (including stored message content used for parsing, where retained)
  • Linked SMS devices, accounts/cards metadata tied to your user ID
  • Import / sync history tied to your account

What may be kept temporarily

  • Short-lived backups or logs needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, typically retained for a limited period and then purged
  • Information we are required by law to keep (if applicable)

Partial data requests

Today, permanent removal of your data is handled through full account deletion via the email process above. In-app actions such as excluding a transaction from spend analytics or removing a card do not delete your entire account.
